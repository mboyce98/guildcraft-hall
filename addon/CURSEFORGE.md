**Description:**

## Bark and Stone

A small helper for guilds leveling together in WoW Forever. Type **/bas** to open it.

### Near my level
Lists guildmates within 4 levels of you, online ones first with the zone they're in, so you can find someone to quest or run dungeons with. Underneath, it shows the dungeons that suit you and how many online guildmates are the right level too.

### Dungeons
Every dungeon from Ragefire Chasm to Blackrock Depths with its level range and location:
- **Green:** right for you.
- **Red:** too hard for now.
- **Grey:** too easy.

Each one lists quests worth picking up before you go, and which online guildmates fit.

### Send to website
Saves your characters, profession skill levels and every recipe they know (open each profession window once). Copy the text and paste it into the Bark and Stone guild website so guildmates can see who crafts what.

### Little extras
- Posts "Ding! Level 24." to guild chat when you level up. Type **/bas ding** to turn it off.
- Reminds you to visit your class trainer on even levels.
- Tells you when a new dungeon fits your level, and when you can learn to ride at 40.

### Commands
- `/bas` opens or closes the window.
- `/bas export` opens Send to website.
- `/bas ding` turns the guild Ding! message on or off.
- `/bas help` lists the commands.
