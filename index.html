Welcome to Bark and Stone

We're an Australian based group looking to form an inclusive and friendly guild. Our goal is to foster a chill, supportive community that can get up to shenanigans and avoid toxicity (looking at you, trade chat). We do eventually want to work towards raids, but our priority is being a social guild first and foremost.

As an inclusive guild, we accept all people regardless of background, identity and orientation.

Guild rules

This is a whole lotta text, and you don't need to memorise everything (aside from Rule 1, really don't break that one). If you feel like something could be an issue, this is your first point of contact. Otherwise, feel free to ask the Guildmaster for clarification.

  1. 1Respect your fellow Guildies

    It doesn't matter if you're an officer or a member: we're all equal, and treating others disrespectfully will not be tolerated. Playful banter is one thing, but respecting others' boundaries is even more important.

    This includes racism, sexism and general bigotry (homophobia, transphobia, etc.). That behaviour will not be tolerated and will get you swiftly kicked from the guild.

  2. 2Keep guild chat family friendly-ish

    NSFW topics are not to be explicitly talked about in guild chats. Imagine the guild is rated M for mature: if a topic would be more at home in an MA or R rated film or game, it's probably best not to talk about it here.

    This ties into Rule 1. If someone is made uncomfortable in chat by a topic, please respect that. You're more than welcome to talk about whatever you like in your DMs.

    Where exactly to draw this line is still being worked out, and this rule will be updated as time goes on.

  3. 3Casual and social, but respect organised events

    This is a casual, social guild. Nobody is forced to take part in any content, keep to any schedule, or be active for any amount of time to stay in the guild.

    That said, if you agree to a raid or similar organised event that takes effort from Guildies, please be respectful of that effort: pay attention to the organisers and keep your attendance up to date. It's totally understandable if you can no longer make it, for whatever reason. Just try to say so quickly, so adjustments can be made if needed :)

  4. 4The Guild Bank

    Once the guild bank is open, it's free real estate for Clansfolk and up. A few things to keep in mind:

    • Only take what you need. Please don't empty out the bank. Use it for gear upgrades, your professions or quests, not to get rich.
    • We don't assume anyone is up to mischief, so the odd stack here or there is no issue (especially for weird quests or recipes). If you're repeatedly taking excessive amounts of things that aren't for your class or professions, you might get a check-in.
    • Don't stress too much about it. As long as you're mindful of other Guildies there will be no issues, and feel free to ask first if you're unsure.
    • Only deposit what you're happy to give to anyone. Once it's in the bank, any member can take it (following the rule above). If you'd rather an item go to someone specific, trade or mail it to them directly to save any confusion.

  5. 5Loot

    For the most part we follow the standard Need or Greed rolls:

    Need
    An upgrade for the character you're currently playing.
    Greed
    For an alt, gold or a profession (e.g. enchanting).

    If rolling isn't enough to settle it, the preference order is:

    1. The organiser (raid leader, or whoever put the party or event together)
    2. Lower level or less geared members
    3. Already better geared members

    That order only applies when rolling doesn't settle it. What you do with loot you rolled for is up to you, as long as you picked Need or Greed honestly. For example, if you needed an item that was an upgrade for your warlock but then gave it to a lower level mage who needed it more, that's totally fine. Just don't Need roll explicitly on behalf of other people.

Consequences

If it gets to the point where a member of leadership has to step in, the standard consequence is a warning, followed by being kicked. Depending on severity, other outcomes include an informal reminder about the rules, demotion, or probation.

If you've been warned, demoted or put on probation and repeat the same or a similar offence, you'll be kicked from the guild. The consequence is at leadership's discretion, but if you feel you've been treated unfairly you can appeal to the Guildmaster. No appeal will be seriously considered if you've clearly broken Rule 1.

Probation

Probation is a role for someone leadership has reason to keep a closer eye on. It doesn't mean they have done anything or will, so treat them with the same respect as every other member. If they break the rules while on probation, less leniency will be given. Otherwise they have the same rights and powers as every other Clansfolk. It probably won't be used often, if at all; we're not in the name-and-shame business, but it's an option.

Warnings and then kicks are the go-to for rule violations. Nobody is above these rules, even the Guildmaster!

The history of the world

  1. Before history

    The Titans shape the world

    Immense, god-like beings called the Titans travelled the cosmos bringing order to young worlds. On Azeroth they tamed the elemental lords and their chaotic servants, sealing them away beneath the earth, and left behind great watchers and keepers to guard their work. Ancient Titan vaults still lie buried in places like Uldaman and Ulduar.

  2. About 10,000 years ago

    The Well of Eternity and the War of the Ancients

    At the heart of the single great continent lay the Well of Eternity, a lake of raw magic. The night elves (the Kaldorei) built a dazzling empire around it under Queen Azshara. Their reckless use of its power drew the attention of Sargeras and his Burning Legion, an army of demons bent on destroying all life.

    Azshara opened the way, and the War of the Ancients began. Led by the demigod Cenarius, the druid Malfurion Stormrage, the priestess Tyrande Whisperwind and the dragons, the defenders won, but the Well imploded. The Great Sundering tore the land apart into the continents we know today and left the swirling Maelstrom in the middle of the sea. You can see it at the centre of the guild map.

    See the Maelstrom on the map

  3. After the Sundering

    A new world tree, and exiles who became the high elves

    The night elves planted a new World Tree at Mount Hyjal and turned to druidism, swearing off arcane magic. Some refused and were exiled. They sailed east, founded Quel'Thalas and became the high elves. In the eastern lands the trolls of the Amani and Gurubashi empires ruled the jungles and forests.

  4. About 2,800 years ago

    The rise of humanity

    Scattered human tribes united as the nation of Arathor, with its capital Strom in what is now the Arathi Highlands. To beat back the trolls they allied with the high elves, who taught a handful of humans to wield magic. Over the centuries Arathor split into the seven human kingdoms, including Stormwind, Lordaeron, Gilneas and the magocracy of Dalaran. The dwarves of Ironforge and the gnomes of Gnomeregan built their own kingdoms in the mountains.

  5. The First War

    The Dark Portal opens

    On the world of Draenor, the shamanistic orc clans were corrupted by the demon Kil'jaeden and the warlock Gul'dan, who forged them into the bloodthirsty Horde. With the help of the possessed guardian Medivh, a Dark Portal was opened into Azeroth, and the Horde poured through into the Swamp of Sorrows and the Blasted Lands. In the war that followed, the kingdom of Stormwind was overrun and burned.

  6. The Second War

    The Alliance of Lordaeron

    Stormwind's refugees fled north. Humans, high elves, dwarves and gnomes joined together as the Alliance of Lordaeron. The Horde, now riding red dragons enslaved at Grim Batol, pushed deep into the north before it was finally broken at Blackrock Spire. The surviving orcs were herded into internment camps, where many fell into a listless stupor.

  7. Between the wars

    Thrall frees the orcs

    A young orc raised as a slave and gladiator, named Thrall, escaped, learned the ways of the shaman and freed his people from the camps. Guided by visions, he led the new Horde west across the sea to Kalimdor, where they befriended the tauren and the Darkspear trolls.

  8. The Third War

    The Scourge and the battle for Mount Hyjal

    A plague swept Lordaeron, turning its people into the undead Scourge, servants of the Lich King. Prince Arthas, trying to stop it, took up the cursed runeblade Frostmourne, murdered his father and delivered his kingdom to the dead. The Burning Legion followed. The demon lord Archimonde marched on the World Tree at Mount Hyjal, and night elves, orcs and humans fought side by side for the first time. The ancient spirits of the forest sacrificed the tree's power to destroy him.

  9. Now: the age of WoW Forever

    An uneasy peace, and old evils stirring

    The Horde has built its capital at Orgrimmar in Durotar. The banshee Sylvanas Windrunner and her Forsaken broke free of the Lich King and hold the Undercity beneath the ruins of Lordaeron. The Alliance rebuilds around Stormwind and Ironforge, while the night elves guard Teldrassil. But the dangers are everywhere:

    • The fire lord Ragnaros stirs beneath Blackrock Mountain. · full loot table
    • The black dragon Onyxia schemes from her lair, and her brother Nefarian builds an army of chromatic dragons. · · full loot table
    • The blood god Hakkar is being called back to Zul'Gurub. · full Zul'Gurub loot table
    • An Old God waits in the desert city of Ahn'Qiraj. · · full loot table

    WoW Forever also opens lands that weren't reachable before, such as Gilneas, the Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Mount Hyjal. Their stories are best discovered in game, together.

The two factions

The Alliance

Bound by old treaties and shared hardship: the humans of Stormwind, the dwarves of Ironforge, the night elves of Darnassus and the gnomes of Gnomeregan. Honour, order and the hope of rebuilding what was lost.

Capitals: Stormwind City, Ironforge, Darnassus

The Horde

A coalition of outsiders who survived by standing together: the orcs of Durotar, the tauren of Mulgore, the Darkspear trolls and the Forsaken. Strength, freedom and a fierce loyalty to one another.

Capitals: Orgrimmar, Thunder Bluff, the Undercity

Classes and races

Pick your hero: what's new in WoW Forever, what each race brings, what each class does, and which races can play which class.

New in WoW Forever

New race

The Skyborne

Elves of Zephras Isle, a storm-wrapped island whose guardian wind spirits have vanished. The only race that can join either faction: pick the High Order (Alliance), descended from Highborne scholars hunting lost arcane lore, or the Windshapers (Horde), heirs of the first elves gifted the wind spirits' skysight.

Starts on Zephras Isle (levels 1 to 12) · Needs the Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher edition

New for the Horde

Undead Paladins

For the first time the Horde has Paladins, and only the Forsaken can be one. They start in Deathknell with their own trainer, follow their own story to Bandarion Keep, and earn their weapon around level 20. They keep Will of the Forsaken, which shrugs off fear, charm and sleep.

Class mounts: Summon Warhorse at 40, Summon Forsaken Charger at 60

Six new pairings

New race and class combinations

  • Alliance Human Hunter
  • Alliance Dwarf Shaman (the Alliance's only Shaman, with new totem quests)
  • Alliance Gnome Priest (with two gnome-only spells)
  • Horde Undead Paladin
  • Horde Orc Mage
  • Horde Troll Warlock

Plus 10 Skyborne pairings. Weapon specialization racials (Human swords, Orc axes) are gone.

Races and their racial traits

Racial traits as reported after Blizzard's late October changes for WoW Forever. Numbers may still be tuned, so check your character's spellbook for the exact values. New marks a trait that's new or changed in Forever. Gold-marked traits help with professions.

Human

Alliance

Adaptable and ambitious, the most numerous people of the Eastern Kingdoms.

  • Perception: briefly see stealthed enemies far more easily.
  • The Human Spirit New: +10% Spirit.
  • Ruthless New: +1% critical strike chance.
  • Will to Survive New: break out of a stun.
  • Diplomacy: faster reputation gains.

Starts in Elwynn Forest

Dwarf

Alliance

Stout miners and smiths of Ironforge, rediscovering their Titan-forged origins.

  • Stoneform: remove poison, disease and bleeding effects.
  • Artisan's Precision New: +1% hit with attacks and spells.
  • Big Game Hunter New: Hunters deal 5% more damage to beasts.
  • Frost Resistance: extra Frost resistance.
  • Treasure Finding: track treasure chests on the minimap.

Starts in Dun Morogh

Night Elf

Alliance

Ancient, nature-bound guardians of Kalimdor who have sworn off arcane magic.

  • Elune's Light New: +10% critical strike chance for 15 seconds.
  • Shadowmeld: vanish while standing still.
  • Quickness: a little extra chance to dodge.
  • Nature Resistance: extra Nature resistance.
  • Wisp Spirit: become a fast wisp when dead.

Starts in Teldrassil

Gnome

Alliance

Brilliant tinkerers, driven from Gnomeregan but never short of ideas.

  • Eureka! New: 10% more damage and cheaper abilities for 8 seconds.
  • Expansive Mind: +5% maximum mana (rage for Warriors).
  • Escape Artist: break free of roots and snares.
  • Engineering Specialization: a head start in Engineering.

Starts in Dun Morogh

Skyborne · High Order

Alliance

Scholarly Skyborne who seek the arcane secrets their Highborne ancestors left behind.

  • Walk on Air New: glide through the air for 10 seconds, steering as you go.
  • Wind Blessed New: +2% haste.
  • Elemental Insight New: +5% damage against elementals.
  • Read Leyline New: +50% health and mana regeneration for 15 seconds. Use it at a leyline to be Attuned for an hour and double the bonus (not in raids).

Starts on Zephras Isle

Orc

Horde

Freed from demonic corruption, a proud warrior people led by Thrall.

  • Blood Fury New: +10% attack power and spell power for 15 seconds, every 2 minutes.
  • Hardiness: shorter stuns.
  • Command: Hunter and Warlock pets deal 5% more damage.

Starts in Durotar

Undead

Horde

The Forsaken: free-willed dead who broke from the Lich King under Sylvanas. In Forever, some take up the Light as Paladins.

  • Will of the Forsaken: break charm, fear and sleep.
  • Cannibalize: recover health from humanoid or undead corpses.
  • Touch of the Grave New: your hits can drain life from the target (every 15 seconds at most).
  • Underwater Breathing: breathe underwater far longer.
  • Shadow Resistance: extra Shadow resistance.

Starts in Tirisfal Glades (Paladins in Deathknell)

Tauren

Horde

Gentle giants of Mulgore who honour the Earth Mother and walk with the land.

  • War Stomp: stun nearby enemies.
  • Endurance: +5% health.
  • Plainsrunning New: you run faster the longer you keep running, up to 60% faster (not while mounted).
  • Nature Resistance: extra Nature resistance.
  • Cultivation: a head start in Herbalism.

Starts in Mulgore

Troll

Horde

The Darkspear tribe, cunning survivors who owe Thrall a life debt.

  • Berserking New: faster attacks and casting for 15 seconds.
  • Regeneration: faster health regeneration, even in combat.
  • Beast Slaying: +5% damage against beasts.

Starts in Durotar

Skyborne · Windshaper

Horde

Skyborne who carry the wind spirits' gift of skysight and ride the storms of Zephras Isle.

  • Walk on Air New: glide through the air for 10 seconds, steering as you go.
  • Wind Blessed New: +2% haste.
  • Elemental Insight New: +5% damage against elementals.
  • Skysight New: +15% movement speed for 15 seconds. Use it at an elemental convergence to be Charged for an hour and double the bonus (not in raids).

Starts on Zephras Isle

The classes

Warrior

TankMelee

Masters of weapons and armor who build Rage by dealing and taking damage. The classic main tank, and a hard-hitting damage dealer.

Mail, then plate at 40 · Rage · Every race

Paladin

HealerTankMelee

Holy warriors with party-wide blessings and auras, strong heals and some of the best survival tools in the game. New in Forever: the Forsaken can be Paladins too, with their own starting story in Deathknell.

Mail, then plate at 40 · Mana · Human, Dwarf, Undead

Hunter

Ranged

Deadly at range with a bow or gun, fighting alongside a tamed beast companion. Great for soloing and pulling in dungeons. New in Forever: Humans and both Skyborne can be Hunters.

Leather, then mail at 40 · Mana · Pet

Rogue

Melee

Stealthy, agile and dangerous. They use Energy and combo points, open locked chests, and craft their own poisons.

Leather · Energy · Lockpicking, poisons

Priest

HealerCaster

The group's lifeline: powerful heals and shields, and the Shadow path for those who prefer to hurt. New in Forever: Gnome Priests, who learn the gnome-only Confounding Flash at 10 and Contingency Plan at 20.

Cloth · Mana · Fortitude buff, resurrection

Shaman

HealerCasterMelee

Speakers to the elements who drop totems to strengthen the whole party. Heal, cast lightning, or fight with an enhanced weapon. New in Forever: Dwarf Shamans bring the class to the Alliance, with all-new totem quests.

Leather, then mail at 40 · Mana · Dwarf, Orc, Tauren, Troll, Windshaper

Mage

Caster

Raw arcane, fire and frost power. Huge damage, crowd control, and the guild's favourite service: conjured food, water and portals. New in Forever: Orc Mages and High Order Skyborne.

Cloth · Mana · Portals, conjured food

Warlock

Caster

Dark casters who command demons, spread curses and drain life. They summon groupmates and make healthstones. New in Forever: Troll Warlocks.

Cloth · Mana · Demon pets, summoning

Druid

HealerTankMeleeCaster

Shapeshifters who can fill any role: bear to tank, cat to fight, tree-hugging healer, or moonkin caster. The game's best all-rounders. New in Forever: both kinds of Skyborne can be Druids.

Leather · Mana, Rage or Energy by form
